Several models mix CC0 and CC BY 4.0 parts, so treat each as CC BY 4.0. The exact licence text for each model is in its `LICENSE.md`, downloaded next to it, and none of the licences cover logos or trademarks.

## Credit line

CC BY 4.0 asks for credit wherever the pictures are used. Where the shop or emails show these room photos, a credit like this is enough (for example on an About or Credits page):

> Room photos include 3D models by Wayfair and the Khronos Group (glTF Sample Assets, CC BY 4.0).
